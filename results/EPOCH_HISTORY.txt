Reading the curve: validation QWK plateaued from epoch 5 onward while
training loss kept falling (0.75 -> 0.53). That gap is the model fitting
the training set harder without generalizing better — i.e. convergence
followed by the onset of mild overfitting, not a bug. Epoch 5's 0.7932 is
this configuration's practical ceiling.

Run configuration
-----------------
  architecture   attention-MIL (gated attention, Ilse et al. 2018)
  backbone       EfficientNet-B0 (ImageNet pretrained)
  tiles/slide    32
  tile size      128 px at level 0
  batch size     6 slides
  eval           8-way dihedral TTA
  stain norm     Macenko, fit per bag
  fold           0 of 5 (stratified by ISUP grade)
  train / val    7301 / 1827 slides
  slide filter   1st-place noisy-label keep-list (9128 of 10616 slides)

What would move the number further (all require a full retrain)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  - more tiles per slide (32 -> 64+); halving them was a speed tradeoff
  - larger tile size or a lower magnification level for more context
  - a bigger backbone than EfficientNet-B0
  - training the remaining folds (1-4) and ensembling the out-of-fold
    predictions — the usual path from a single-fold result into the
    0.83-0.87 range

For context: the PANDA competition winner scored 0.9408 on the private
leaderboard using a large multi-model ensemble. Independent external
validation of the best algorithms on non-PANDA cohorts landed at
0.862-0.868, and pathologist inter-rater agreement is 0.907-0.926
(Bulten et al. 2022, Nature Medicine). A single fold at 0.79 is a
legitimate result, not a disappointing one.
